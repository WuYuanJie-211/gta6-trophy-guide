      About this guide

      This is an unofficial, fan-made trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, PlayStation, or Take-Two Interactive. All trophy names, descriptions and grades are official data, used for reference. No Rockstar artwork, logos or game assets are included — everything here is original text and code.

      How the guidance was written

      • Every trophy has a guide in the author's own words — how to do it, where, and when it can be missed.
      • Guidance is sourced from published guides (PowerPyx, PlayStationTrophies.org, PSNProfiles, TrueTrophies, Exophase) and cross-checked against the official trophy descriptions. No other guide's text was copied.
      • 4 trophies had no public guide at all when this was built (GTA VI launched 19 November 2026). They are marked honestly as “No public guide yet” instead of guessing — see the list below.
      • The tracker stores your progress in your browser's localStorage only. Nothing leaves your device. Export / Import moves progress as a JSON file.
